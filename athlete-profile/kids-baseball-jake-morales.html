MEET JAKE

Jake "Slugger" Morales has been swinging for the fences since tee-ball. A lightning-quick shortstop with a big bat, he's the Riverside Rockets' spark plug — first to practice, last to leave, and always cheering on his teammates.

When he's not on the diamond, Jake and his family post game highlights and practice tips to help other young players fall in love with the game.

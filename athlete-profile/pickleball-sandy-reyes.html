MEET SANDY

Sandy "The Dink" Reyes picked up a paddle at 42 and never looked back. A crafty 4.5 amateur known for soft hands and sneaky angles, she's become a fixture on the Austin rec circuit — and a favorite doubles partner.

She shares matches, paddle reviews, and beginner-friendly drills to grow her local club and welcome new players to the game.

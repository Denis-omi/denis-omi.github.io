The Story

Damon "Blackout" Rivera turned a scrappy Miami boxing gym into a launchpad for one of the most feared finishers in the lightweight division. Known for switching the lights out early, he blends counter-striking precision with a bottomless gas tank and a showman's flair that spills straight onto his streams.

Off the canvas, Rivera has built a media machine — training vlogs, live gaming nights, and unfiltered interviews — turning fans into a movement 11 million strong.

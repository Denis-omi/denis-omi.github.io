The Story

Kai "Avalanche" Sorensen grew up lapping the Innsbruck parks before the lifts opened, chasing bigger jumps and cleaner grabs. A fearless big-air specialist, he's rewritten what's possible off the booter — and films every progression session for the fans.

Between contests he runs a backcountry film crew and a nonstop stream schedule, turning 7 million followers into front-row seats for every send.
